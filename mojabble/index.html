MojAbble

Mahjong × Scrabble

A word videogame that fuses Mahjong Solitaire tile layouts with Scrabble letter scoring. Pull free tiles off the board, build words, chain combos, and climb the global leaderboard. Free in your browser, no install, no account.

Play Now Get it on iOS & Android

The Videogame

Every board is a Mahjong Solitaire layout stacked with letter tiles. Only free tiles can be taken, so every word you spell opens and closes routes through the stack. Scoring follows Scrabble letter values with combo multipliers for consecutive valid words. Words are checked against the full SOWPODS / Collins tournament dictionary: 267,627 words, 3 letters and up, no length cap.

Tournament Dictionary

The complete SOWPODS / Collins Scrabble Words list. If it plays in tournament Scrabble, it plays here.

Combos & Multipliers

Consecutive valid words build a combo chain. Longer and rarer words score bigger bonuses.

Difficulty Tiers

Layouts range from gentle turtles to brutal towers. Pick your poison.

Seeded Multiplayer Boards

Share a room code and race friends on the exact same board with the same tile draw.

Global Leaderboard

High scores and rare-word finds persist to a global board. Bragging rights included.

Instant & Lightweight

Vanilla JS, Canvas 2D, and procedural Web Audio. Zero dependencies, loads in seconds.

How It Plays

Scan the stack. Tiles are free when they have an open side and nothing on top, just like Mahjong Solitaire.

Pull letters. Click free tiles to move them to your rack. Choose carefully, digging changes what opens next.

Spell words. Submit any word of 3+ letters. The dictionary is the judge.

Chain combos. Back-to-back valid words multiply your score. Clear the board for the full bonus.
